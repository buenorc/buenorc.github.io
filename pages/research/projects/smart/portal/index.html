The concept

From reactive monitoring to predictive limnology

Reservoir management in Brazil still relies largely on the past — sparse field campaigns and models applied retrospectively. This project builds a digital twin: a continuously updated virtual replica of Passaúna that reproduces its current state, learns from observed data, and predicts its future thermal structure, circulation and water quality.

Sense

A cascading LoRa network of low-cost Heltec WiFi LoRa nodes streams water & air temperature, humidity and water level from across the reservoir in near real time.

Two complementary projects, one platform

SMART & LAKE TWIN

SMART

Sistema de Monitoramento Avançado de Reservatórios e Tributários. An intelligent, economical IoT monitoring network for rivers and reservoirs. Low-cost LoRa sensors measure water level, temperature, turbidity, pH, chlorophyll, conductivity and meteorology, feeding an online platform with machine-learning analysis accessible to the public and to water authorities.

LAKE TWIN

Digital twins for forecasting the hydrodynamics and water quality of tropical reservoirs. Combines the monitoring network with hierarchical hydrodynamic modelling, near-real-time data assimilation and machine learning, plus Interwave Analyzer indicators (thermocline depth, Schmidt stability, Lake number, internal-wave modes) to anticipate change.

How the twin stays in sync

The operational forecast cycle

Force

Fetch global-model forecasts — wind, solar radiation, air temperature, rainfall (GFS / Open-Meteo) and river inflow (GloFAS).

Simulate

Run GLM & Delft3D forward from the last state to predict the next window (e.g. every 50 h).

Assimilate

Blend fresh sensor observations into the model state with an Ensemble Kalman Filter to correct drift.

Publish

Push updated fields & indicators to this portal — maps, profiles, time series and alerts.
