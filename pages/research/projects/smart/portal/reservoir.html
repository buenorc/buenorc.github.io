The basin

The Passaúna river basin lies in the western portion of the Curitiba metropolitan region, within the Upper Iguaçu basin. The river runs about 57 km, with headwaters in the Serras de São Luiz do Purunã and Bocaina near 1,040 m elevation. A dam built in the late 1980s for public supply formed the reservoir in 1990. Today it supplies more than 650,000 inhabitants.

Why its dynamics matter

Stratification

Warm, subtropical climate drives seasonal thermal stratification — strong in summer, weakening toward winter mixing — which governs vertical transport of oxygen and nutrients.

Circulation & internal waves

Wind forcing generates basin-scale internal seiches and upwelling that can rapidly redistribute heat and dissolved properties along the elongated basin.

Water quality

These physical processes set the stage for algal blooms and oxygen depletion — the conditions the twin is built to anticipate.
